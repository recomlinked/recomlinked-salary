About Recomlinked

Built for the conversation
most people never prepare for

Whether you're negotiating a raise at your current job or countering a new offer, the gap between what you earn and what you're worth is almost never a skills problem — it's a preparation problem.

Most people who ask for more money and don't get it didn't fail because they weren't good enough. They failed because they walked in — or sent the email — without a script, without a plan, and without having considered a single pushback response. That's the problem we solve.

Why we built this

The advice was everywhere. The preparation wasn't.

There's no shortage of articles telling you to "know your worth" or "pick the right time." That advice is fine. It doesn't help you when your manager says the budget is frozen and you freeze up — or when you get a job offer and aren't sure if $95,000 should become $103,000 before you sign.

We built two tools to solve the preparation problem, not the information problem. We don't just tell you what to say. We build a personalized plan, simulate the conversation, and make sure nothing surprises you when you're actually in the room or hitting send.

What makes us different

Two products. One mission. Zero generic advice.

  • Raise Plan — for your current job Answer questions about your evidence, timing, manager, company, and market position. Get a personalized raise strategy, a practice simulation, and a pushback playbook — not a generic script anyone could have downloaded.
  • Counter Offer — for a new offer Answer 15 questions about your offer, leverage, deadline, and risk style. Get your exact negotiation room, a safe/moderate/ambitious counter range, a ready-to-send email, a pushback playbook, and fallback responses.
  • Practice until it's familiar The simulation mode lets you run the conversation with an AI that plays your manager — supportive, neutral, or resistant — so the real conversation feels like one you've already had.
  • One price, yours to keep No subscription. No monthly fee. Pay once and your full plan stays with you for 30 days while you prepare and have the conversation.
