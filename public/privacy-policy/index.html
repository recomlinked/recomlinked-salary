Recomlinked Technologies Inc. ("Recomlinked", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Salary Negotiation Coach at salary.recomlinked.com, how we use it, and what rights you have.

We do not collect payment card information directly. All payment processing is handled by Stripe.

2 How we use your information

  • Delivering your plan — your negotiation profile is sent to the Anthropic Claude API to generate your personalized 12-section raise plan, opening scripts, and pushback playbook.
  • Session continuity — your plan and coaching history are stored so you can return to your session on any device during your 30-day access window.
  • Access management — your email is used to issue, verify, and re-send your access token.
  • Welcome and support emails — we send one welcome email at purchase and respond to support requests. We do not send marketing emails unless you explicitly opt in.
  • Product improvement — anonymized, aggregated usage data helps us improve the product.

3 Third-party services

We work with the following trusted providers. Each processes data on our behalf under their own privacy obligations:

  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — your negotiation profile and coaching messages are sent to Anthropic's API to generate plan content and coaching responses. Anthropic does not use API inputs to train their models by default. See anthropic.com/privacy.
  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe collects and stores your payment details. We receive only a payment confirmation and your email and name. See stripe.com/privacy.
  • Upstash (Redis) — your user record, plan content, and session data are stored in Upstash's managed Redis database. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (welcome email, magic links).
  • Vercel — serverless hosting for the application and API.
  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymized usage analytics. IP addresses are anonymized. You can opt out via browser settings or Google's opt-out tool.
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymized session recording and heatmaps to understand how users navigate the product.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

4 Payments & Stripe

All transactions are processed by Stripe, Inc. We never see or store your full card number, CVV, or billing address. Stripe handles PCI-DSS compliance on our behalf. After purchase, we receive a confirmation with your email, name, and the amount charged.

Refund requests can be made at any time by emailing support@recomlinked.com.

5 Your data and AI processing

To generate your personalized negotiation plan, we send the following to Anthropic's Claude API:

  • Your answers to the 5 discovery questions (role, evidence, manager type, company situation, market position)
  • Your blocker — the specific challenge you want to address in the negotiation
  • In coaching mode: your messages and the conversation history

We do not send your name, email, or payment information to the AI. Anthropic's API usage policy prohibits them from using API inputs to train models without explicit consent. Your negotiation details are not used to train AI models.

6 Data retention

Your user record, plan content, and coaching history are stored for 30 days from the date of purchase — matching your access window. After 30 days, your data is automatically deleted from our servers.

If you request deletion before the 30-day window, we will delete your data within 24 hours. You can trigger this yourself from the paid dashboard, or by emailing support@recomlinked.com.

Anonymized, aggregated analytics data (no personal identifiers) may be retained indefinitely for product improvement.

Request a copy of all data we hold about you.

Request deletion of all your personal data at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, use the data export/delete tools in your paid dashboard, or contact us at the address below.

8 Security

We use industry-standard technical measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption in transit — all data between your browser and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encryption at rest — your data in Upstash Redis is encrypted at rest.
  • Token-based access — your coaching session is protected by a cryptographically random 32-character access token, not a password. The token expires automatically after 30 days.
  • No password storage — we don't use passwords. There's no password database to breach.

9 Cookies & analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

  • Session state — your plan and paid status are stored in your browser's localStorage, not in cookies. This data stays on your device.
  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymized usage data. No personal identifiers are passed to Google. You can opt out using Google's browser add-on.
  • Microsoft Clarity — session recordings are anonymized and do not include personally identifiable information.

We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertising networks.
